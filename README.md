# big-tech-stock-analysis-r
# 📊 Big Tech Stock Analysis (R Project)

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes stock price trends of major technology companies using R.
It focuses on data cleaning, transformation, and visualization to uncover insights from historical stock data.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze stock price trends over time
* Compare performance of major tech companies
* Visualize relationships between stock price and trading volume

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* R
* tidyverse
* ggplot2
* dplyr
* lubridate

## 📂 Dataset

* Big Tech stock price dataset
* Company information dataset

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 1. Data Preprocessing

* Merged stock and company datasets
* Converted date formats
* Created new features:

  * Mean stock price
  * Mean high-low price

### 2. Visualizations

* 📈 Line Plot: Stock price trends over time
* 📦 Box Plot: Yearly price distribution of major companies
* 🔵 Scatter Plot: Stock price vs trading volume
* 📊 Area Plot: Volume trends over time

## 📈 Key Insights

* Identified trends in stock growth across major tech companies
* Compared volatility between companies
* Observed relationship between stock price and trading volume

## 🚀 Future Improvements

* Add predictive modeling (time series forecasting)
* Improve data filtering and accuracy
* Interactive dashboards (Shiny / Tableau)
